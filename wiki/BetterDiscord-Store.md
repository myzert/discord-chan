# ⚡ BetterDiscord Addon Store & Downloader

Discord-Chan Studio is **Powered by the BetterDiscord Engine**. It features a built-in **Addon Store & UI Downloader** allowing users to browse, download, and install popular community themes and plugins directly into BetterDiscord with 1-click.

## 📥 Direct Link Downloader

You can also paste ANY `.theme.css` or `.plugin.js` direct URL (e.g., from GitHub raw or BetterDiscord community repositories) into the Direct Link Downloader box inside Discord-Chan Studio. The app will automatically download and place it in the correct BetterDiscord folder (`themes/` or `plugins/`).

After installing any theme or plugin, press **`Ctrl + R`** in Discord to reload the client and activate your new add-on!
