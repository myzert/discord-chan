# 📦 Export & Import Theme Bundles (.zip)

Easily share your personalized Discord theme configurations with the community.

### 📤 Exporting:
1. Customize your colors, corner radius, translucency, and wallpapers in Studio.
2. Go to `#  export-import-themes`.
3. Click **[Export Theme (.zip)]**.
4. The generated `.zip` bundle includes:
   - `config.json` (all slider values, hex codes, and settings)
   - `theme.css` (compiled CSS stylesheet)
   - `meta.json` (theme name, author, and timestamp)
   - Embedded high-res wallpaper image file

### 📥 Importing:
1. Download any `.zip` theme shared by a friend or community member.
2. In Studio, click **[Import Theme (.zip)...]**.
3. Studio extracts the wallpaper into `assets/wallpapers/imported/`, updates your settings, and immediately applies the theme to Discord!
